

 1. What I Asked the AI
I asked the AI to help me create an HTML page explaining how to use a generator safely.

 2. What the AI Provided
The AI provided an HTML example with a main heading, section headings, an unordered list, an ordered list, and semantic HTML elements.

 3. What I Changed and Why
I reviewed the example and checked that the instructions were suitable for the intended reader. I used `<strong>` to mark important safety warnings, `<em>` to emphasise a word, and `<abbr>` to explain the abbreviation kVA.

 4. Validation
Validation result: shows no error and no warning signs]

 5. What I Learned
I learned how HTML elements communicate meaning, and why important warnings should be marked with `<strong>` rather than merely made bold.
