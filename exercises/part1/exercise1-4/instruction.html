How to Use a Generator Safely

These instructions are for people responsible for a generator. Follow the manufacturer's instructions and keep other people away from the equipment.

What You Need

Before starting, make sure the equipment is suitable for the task.

  • The generator's manufacturer's manual
  • Dry, stable outdoor ground
  • A suitable location away from doors and windows
  • Assistance from a qualified adult or technician when needed

Steps

Follow these safety steps in order.

  1. Keep the generator outdoors. Never operate it inside a house, room, garage or enclosed space.
  2. Place it well away from doors, windows and air vents, following the manufacturer's safety distances.
  3. Check the manual and inspect the equipment for visible damage.
  4. Have a qualified adult or technician handle operation and electrical connections if you are not trained.
  5. Follow the manufacturer's shutdown instructions before refuelling or handling the equipment.

If Something Goes Wrong

If you smell fuel, see a fuel leak, or notice damaged electrical parts, do not start or continue using the generator. Move away and tell a responsible adult or qualified technician. If anyone feels dizzy or unwell near a generator, move to fresh air and seek urgent medical help. Carbon monoxide is a dangerous gas that you cannot see or smell.

Read the manufacturer's instructions carefully. Use the generator outdoors, not indoors. Any power rating marked in kVA should be checked against the equipment manual.
