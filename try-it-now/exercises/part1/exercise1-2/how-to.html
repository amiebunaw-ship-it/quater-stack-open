How to Prepare for an Examination

Examination preparation helps students understand their subjects and organise their time. Good preparation can also help students feel more confident when answering questions.

Make a Study Plan

A study plan helps students organise their subjects and use their available study time wisely.

List Your Subjects

Write down all the subjects you need to study and identify the topics that require more attention.

Prepare a Timetable

Set aside time for each subject. Include short breaks so that you can maintain your concentration.

Gather Your Study Materials

Collect your notebooks, textbooks, class notes and past examination questions before beginning your revision. Keeping these materials together makes studying easier.

Revise Your Lessons

Read your notes carefully and practise answering questions. Try to explain important concepts in your own words to check whether you understand them.

Prepare for Examination Day

Confirm the examination date, arrange the materials you are allowed to bring, and plan to arrive at the examination venue on time. Get enough rest before the examination.
